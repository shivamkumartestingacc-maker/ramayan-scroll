Ravana had conquered the heavens.

Indra was defeated, the gods were scattered, and the sky itself went dark.

Defeated, Indra came seeking refuge.

He joined his hands and called to the One beyond all worlds.

And the ocean of stars began to stir.

Beneath the endless night, the cosmic ocean rose in slow, shining waves.

Ananta rose, the serpent without end.

His hoods opened over the waters, a canopy for the One who was coming.

His Sudarshana blazed to life.

The discus no darkness can withstand, turning on the finger of the Lord.

From his stillness, a lotus opened.

Pure and untouched, the seat from which all creation unfolds.

And Narayana answered.

Protector of the three worlds, he revealed himself in every star.

He who answers every prayer.

Om Namo Narayanaya
